The Chrome Extension

Add anything from anywhere, in one click.

This is the small magic trick. Once installed, a little button sits in your browser. You're browsing a beautiful crib on a Danish design site, you click the button, it goes onto your registry. Same for Etsy, the brand's own DTC site, a small shop you stumbled into — anywhere.

  1. Open Chrome on a computer. The extension only installs on desktop Chrome — not on your phone. Once installed, your registry stays in sync across devices.
  2. Visit the Chrome Web Store page. Go to chromewebstore.google.com and search for "Add to Zola Baby Button". Make sure it's the Baby one, not the wedding version.
  3. Click "Add to Chrome." A small popup will confirm — click "Add extension." It installs in a few seconds.
  4. Pin it to your toolbar. Click the little puzzle-piece icon in the top-right of Chrome. Find "Add to Zola Baby Button" in the list and click the pin icon next to it so it stays visible.
  5. Sign in once. Click the new icon. It'll ask you to sign in with your Zola credentials. You should only need to do this the first time (in theory — see note below).
  6. Try it out. Go to any product page on the internet. Click the icon. A small panel opens, you can edit the product name and details, then click "Add to Registry." Done.
If the extension acts up: Zola has a backup method. On any product page, copy the URL, then in your Zola registry go to "Add Gifts" → "Add from Another Store" and paste the link there. Same result, two more clicks.
